## General Reminders

- **All premiums are estimates.** Derived from typical IV ranges; verify against live options chains before trading. Premiums fluctuate with IV daily.
- **Use limit orders** at or near mid-market. Never sell at the bid on thinly traded names (BRK.B, VLY).
- **Rolling:** If a position moves sharply above the strike near expiry, consider rolling the call out one month rather than taking assignment or buying to close at a loss.
- **Tax:** Covered calls on positions held less than 1 year can affect qualified dividend and long-term capital gains treatment. Consult a tax advisor if any of these positions are near the 1-year mark.
- **This is not financial advice.** Verify all data and consult your broker or financial advisor before executing any trades.
